Print Equivalent

For A4 brochures, proposals, brand books:

Role	Size	Leading
Hero	48–72pt	50–70pt
Heading 1	36pt	40pt
Heading 2	24pt	30pt
Heading 3	16pt	22pt
Body	10.5–11pt	16pt
Caption	8–9pt	12pt

Swiss design often uses smaller body text with more whitespace rather than making everything large.

Letter Spacing Rules

Swiss typography is very controlled.

Large headings

Tight:

-20 to -40 tracking in Illustrator

or:

-0.02em to -0.04em web

Example:

PROJECT PARTNERSHIP

Feels premium when slightly compressed.

Body text

Usually:

0 tracking
Labels / Navigation

Increase spacing:

+50 to +150 tracking in Illustrator

Example:

ABOUT
PROJECTS
CONTACT
