ABOUT NORTH COLLECTIVE

A DIFFERENT APPROACH TO PROJECT DELIVERY

NORTH COLLECTIVE WAS CREATED AROUND A SIMPLE BELIEF:

North Collective was created around a simple belief: successful projects require more than products. They require the right relationships, the right strategy, and the right execution.

Our role is to help clients source smarter, build better, and grow stronger by providing access to trusted suppliers, experienced project partners, and practical solutions that create long-term value.

TRANSPARENT PROCUREMENT.
TRUSTED PARTNERSHIPS.

At North Collective, we believe successful projects are built on trust, transparency, and long-term relationships. We work collaboratively with a carefully selected network of preferred vendors and manufacturing partners, delivering value while maintaining flexibility and objectivity.

Integrity is at the core of everything we do. We believe clients deserve clear communication, transparent pricing structures, and honest recommendations that support the best interests of every project.

By combining procurement expertise with strategic supplier relationships and a commitment to accountability, North Collective provides a procurement process that is straightforward, transparent, and focused on delivering measurable value.

FOUNDERS

At North Collective, partners Leith MacRobert and Sara Nicholson combine their experience, industry knowledge, and shared passion for beautiful spaces to create a seamless experience for their clients. Their complementary strengths and collaborative approach make them a trusted team, dedicated to delivering exceptional service and successful projects from start to finish.


Meet Leith

Director of Business Development

Leith MacRobert has been servicing the hospitality industry since he immigrated to Canada from South Africa 20 years ago. He has vast experience supplying products to designers, owners and operators of commercial and hospitality projects across North America.

Over the years, Leith has helped develop brand standards for a number of leading hotel and restaurant chains, bringing practical experience and a deep understanding of what it takes to deliver successful projects.

Leith currently sits on the board of directors for Vancouver Tourism and Hospitality. His expansive industry network, deep market knowledge, and long-standing supplier relationships enable him to connect clients with trusted partners and innovative solutions, ensuring every project is delivered with value and efficiency.

Meet Sara

Director of Operations

Sara Nicholson developed a passion for the industry at a young age, growing up in her fathers millwork shop. She went on to build a career in the industry, spending more than a decade working closely with the design community while developing extensive knowledge of products, materials, and the procurement process.

With experience both supporting clients directly and through distribution channels, Sara brings a well-rounded perspective to every project. Her broad product knowledge allows her to identify practical, cost-effective solutions and provide valuable insight throughout the design and procurement process. Known for her collaborative approach and attention to detail, Sara helps clients make informed decisions while keeping projects on track and aligned with their goals.
