ABOUT NORTH COLLECTIVE

A DIFFERENT APPROACH TO PROJECT DELIVERY

NORTH COLLECTIVE WAS CREATED AROUND A SIMPLE BELIEF:

North Collective was created around a simple belief: successful projects require more than products. They require the right relationships, the right strategy, and the right execution.

Our role is to help clients source smarter, build better, and grow stronger by providing access to trusted suppliers, experienced project partners, and practical solutions that create long-term value.

TRANSPARENT PROCUREMENT.
TRUSTED PARTNERSHIPS.

At North Collective, we believe successful projects are built on trust, transparency, and long-term relationships. We work collaboratively with a carefully selected network of preferred vendors and manufacturing partners, delivering value while maintaining flexibility and objectivity.

Integrity is at the core of everything we do. We believe clients deserve clear communication, transparent pricing structures, and honest recommendations that support the best interests of every project.

By combining procurement expertise with strategic supplier relationships and a commitment to accountability, North Collective provides a procurement process that is straightforward, transparent, and focused on delivering measurable value.

FOUNDERS

Leith MacRobert

Director of Business Development

Leith MacRobert brings more than 20 years of experience in hospitality, commercial interiors, procurement, supplier representation, and business development across Canada and the United States.

Throughout his career, Leith has worked closely with hotel ownership groups, developers, operators, designers, and procurement teams, supporting projects ranging from renovations and brand conversions to new construction and large-scale capital improvement initiatives.

Known for his strong industry relationships and strategic approach, Leith has generated millions of dollars in annual sales while helping clients navigate complex procurement decisions, supplier partnerships, budgeting, and project execution. His experience extends beyond procurement to include strategic sourcing, brand standards, supplier development, and commercial growth initiatives.

As Director of Business Development at North Collective, Leith leads the company's business development strategy, supplier partnerships, procurement initiatives, and client relationships.

Working closely with developers, ownership groups, operators, and project teams, he helps clients identify opportunities, develop sourcing strategies, and successfully deliver commercial and hospitality projects from planning through completion.

Sara Phillips

Director of Operations

Sara brings more than 15 years of experience in the furnishings and finishes sector, specialising in hospitality and commercial projects. Her career has spanned project coordination, business development, supply chain management, and distribution, providing her with a well-rounded understanding of the industry and the unique challenges faced by owners, operators, and design teams.

Throughout her career, Sara has worked closely with leading hospitality brands, supporting renovations, property improvement plans, brand conversions, and new-build developments across North America. She has also collaborated with clients to develop new brand standards that have been successfully implemented nationwide.

As Director of Operations at North Collective, Sara works closely with designers, developers, ownership groups, and operators to bring projects to life from concept through completion. Her ability to build strong supplier relationships, identify strategic sourcing opportunities, and proactively solve complex project challenges enables her to deliver value for every client.

Sara is passionate about creating exceptional spaces and fostering long-term partnerships, making her a trusted collaborator throughout every stage of the project lifecycle.

In addition to her extensive hospitality experience, Sara brings a strong background in supply chain management and distribution. She successfully managed a network of retail locations during the rollout of a major flooring brand, doubling revenue across her region within two years.
