ABOUT NORTH COLLECTIVE

BUILT ON EXPERIENCE.

DRIVEN BY RELATIONSHIPS.

"Successful projects require more than products. They require the right relationships, the right strategy and the right execution."

North Collective is a boutique commercial procurement and project solutions firm built on decades of experience across Canada's hospitality and commercial sectors.
Through our experience of delivering projects alongside Canada's leading commercial brands, we have cultivated a trusted network of suppliers, consultants, project professionals, and strategic partners. North Collective brings that expertise together under one roof, giving clients a single point of contact to source products, coordinate services, and move projects forward with clarity and confidence.
From estimating and procurement to project management, owner representation, and value engineering, we offer everything from individual services to fully integrated turnkey solutions.
